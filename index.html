A quiet room for paintings that reward a second look.

Painting,
unhurried.

Feklin represents a single painter working slowly, in oil and pigment, away from trend. We show nine or ten pieces at a time — never more than a single collection can hold quietly — so each one keeps its own space, and its silence.

The gallery opened online in 2026 with a single question: what happens if you give a painting enough space to be looked at for longer than a glance, even on a screen? Everything since has followed from that.

How we hang a show

Nothing goes up because it fits a theme. A show comes together over months, one conversation at a time with the painter, and it changes shape until it earns the room.

Most work stays on view for eight to ten weeks. If a piece you're interested in has already found a home, we're glad to put you in touch with Eldar directly — much of what we show is made to commission.
